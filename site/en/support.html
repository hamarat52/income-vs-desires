Support

A price didn't convert, something went wrong, or you'd like a feature? Pick one of the ways below.

Contact us

  • GitHub Issues: github.com/hamarat52/income-vs-desires/issues — public; you need a GitHub account to post.
  • Email: hermes.agent.hamarat@gmail.com
  • From inside the extension: the "Report a bug / request a feature" form on the Expenses page prepares the email for you; you see the full text before you send it.

What helps us fix it quickly

  • The page address (the site where prices didn't convert)
  • Browser and device: Chrome or Safari; Mac, iPhone or iPad
  • Extension version (shown in the report preview on the Expenses page)
  • What you expected and what happened; a screenshot if you can

There's no need to tell us your pay or your expenses. The report the extension prepares doesn't include them either.

Common questions

Prices didn't turn into time. In the toolbar panel, check that your pay is entered, the extension is on and it isn't turned off for this site; then press "Rescan this page". The extension recognises prices in TRY, USD, EUR and GBP.

How do I turn it on in Safari? Mac: open the app once, then enable IncomeVsDesires in Safari > Settings > Extensions and allow it on websites. iPhone and iPad: turn it on in Settings > Apps > Safari > Extensions and allow it on websites.

What we do with your data (in short: nothing): privacy policy.
