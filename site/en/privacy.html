Privacy policy

The short version: we collect no data. No server, no account, no analytics.

What data we handle

The IncomeVsDesires extension stores the following only in your browser:

  • The monthly take-home pay and currency you enter
  • Your work schedule (hours per day, days per month)
  • The fixed expenses you add (name, amount, period, type)
  • The list of sites where you turned the extension off

In Chrome, this data is kept in Chrome's own storage.sync storage. If sync is turned on in your Chrome account, Google syncs it on its own infrastructure; that is Chrome's standard behavior and it never reaches us.

In Safari (Mac, iPhone, iPad), the same data is kept in Safari's extension storage; it never reaches us.

What we don't collect

  • The sites you visit, your history, your tabs
  • The prices you see or convert
  • IP address, device ID, cookies
  • Any personally identifiable information

The extension has no server. Since there is nowhere to send your data, we couldn't collect it even if we wanted to.

Outgoing requests

Once a day, the extension fetches exchange rates from frankfurter.dev (European Central Bank data). If that address can't be reached, it asks for the same table from two backup sources in turn: open.er-api.com and currency-api (via cdn.jsdelivr.net). These requests only ask for the rate table; they contain nothing about your pay, the sites you visit or anything that identifies you. If none of them responds, the extension uses the last rates it fetched successfully and says so in the panel.

What we do to pages

The extension finds price text on the page you are viewing and writes working time in its place. This happens entirely inside the browser, on the page being displayed. Page content is never sent anywhere or saved.

On some domains the extension doesn't run at all (such as email, document and banking sites). You can also turn it off for any site with one click from the panel.

If you send feedback

If you use the "report a bug" form inside the extension, you see the text exactly as it will be sent before you send it. The report includes the extension version, browser information, your currency and your work schedule. Your pay and your expense list are never included.

This website

This website uses no cookies, has no analytics script and loads no third-party resources. The calculator on the page runs entirely in your browser; the pay you type goes nowhere.

The only thing we keep in your browser is the theme you pick with the button in the top bar (ivd-theme). It is never sent to a server and doesn't identify you; its only job is to open the page in the colors you chose next time.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above is updated. If a change would start collecting data, it will be stated clearly in the extension's release notes.
